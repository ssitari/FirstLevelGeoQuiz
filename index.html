    How to play

    A first-level administrative unit — a state, province, region, prefecture, oblast — is shown in silhouette, north up. Name it.

    • Novice — pick one country; guess among its units.
    • Intermediate — the whole world, weighted toward recognizable units.
    • Ironman — uniformly random from all 4,000+ units. Good luck.
    • Daily — five fixed puzzles, same for everyone, shareable.

    Each wrong guess reveals the next hint but costs points. Buying a hint early costs more. Solve with no hints to build a streak multiplier.
